# Practice Tasks for Chapter 1

**Task type:** formal problem statement and a verbal description of the algorithm.

For each task, write up **four items**:
1. **Given** — input data with constraints;
2. **Find** — output data;
3. **Model** — formulas;
4. **Algorithm** — a numbered verbal description (no programming language);
5. **Tests** — at least 3 sets, including one edge case.

---

### Task 1.1
Compute the perimeter and area of a rectangle from its two sides.

### Task 1.2
Compute the circumference of a circle and the area of a disk from the radius.

### Task 1.3
Compute the volume and total surface area of a rectangular cuboid
from its three dimensions.

### Task 1.4
Compute the arithmetic mean of three real numbers.

### Task 1.5
Convert a temperature from degrees Celsius to degrees Fahrenheit.

### Task 1.6
Compute the distance between two points on a plane from their coordinates.

### Task 1.7
Compute the distance a car travels in time t at speed v,
and the fuel consumption if q liters are used per 100 km.

### Task 1.8
Compute the amount of a deposit after one year with simple interest:
the initial amount and the annual rate in percent are given.

### Task 1.9
Compute the area of a triangle from its three sides (Heron's formula).
State the constraints on the input data.

### Task 1.10
Compute the cost of a purchase: the unit price, the quantity,
and the discount in percent are given.

---

## Report Format (sample for Task 1.1)

```
Given:   a > 0, b > 0 — sides of the rectangle (real numbers)
Find:    P — perimeter, S — area
Model:   P = 2*(a + b);  S = a * b
Algorithm:
  1. Input a.
  2. Input b.
  3. P ← 2 * (a + b)
  4. S ← a * b
  5. Output P, S.
  6. End.
Tests:   (3, 4) → P=14, S=12
         (1, 1) → P=4,  S=1
         (0.5, 100) → P=201, S=50
```
